# Candidates — The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

The pitch: **one sentence, ≤ 240 characters**, pitching the worst, most useless app
imaginable. The judge (a deadpan product critic) compares entries pairwise and weighs,
in this order:

1. **Specific app + useless function** — names a specific app and what it uselessly does, in one sentence.
2. **Funny uselessness** — absurd logic, deadpan confidence, or a perfect mismatch between a problem nobody has and the app that solves it.
3. **Real-pitch sound** — a product name, a clear statement of what it does and who it is for.
4. **Clean use of the budget** — one clean sentence, no padding, no second idea crowding it.
5. **Vivid picture** — a specific scene in the reader's head, not a generic joke about apps.

Scoring below: each criterion 0–5, weighted ×5/×4/×3/×2/×1 in the judge's priority
order (max 75). Character counts include the final period.

## Tournament — top 8, judged pairwise in the brief's criterion order

Seeds by score: #1 UmbrellaDry (75), #6 QueueMe (74), #4 PuddlePremium (72),
#9 PlantWhisper Premium (72), #36 DoorknobWarmth (71), #3 BlinkLog (68),
#16 YawnChain (68), #23 CloudShape Insurance (68).

### Quarter-finals

- **#1 UmbrellaDry vs #23 CloudShape Insurance → UmbrellaDry.** Tied on C1–C2;
  C3 decides it: UmbrellaDry names exactly who it is for, CloudShape never says.
- **#4 PuddlePremium vs #3 BlinkLog → PuddlePremium.** C2: reserving a puddle with
  surge pricing is the more absurd mechanism; a printed blink report is merely bureaucratic.
- **#6 QueueMe vs #16 YawnChain → QueueMe.** C3: QueueMe carries a price and a real
  subscription shape; YawnChain's "who it is for" stays implicit.
- **#9 PlantWhisper Premium vs #36 DoorknobWarmth → PlantWhisper Premium.** C2 by a
  wide margin: an inbox the plants cannot open, read, or photosynthesize beats a
  doorknob-temperature newsletter, however deadpan.

### Semi-finals

- **#1 UmbrellaDry vs #4 PuddlePremium → UmbrellaDry.** C2 is close (both absurd),
  C5 decides: a person emotionally checking on their one umbrella is a sharper
  picture than a reserved puddle.
- **#6 QueueMe vs #9 PlantWhisper Premium → QueueMe.** C3: the $4.99/month price and
  the after-checkout timing make it sound like a pitch a founder would defend in a
  meeting; PlantWhisper is funnier (C2) but QueueMe's real-pitch shape differs by more.

### Final

- **#1 UmbrellaDry vs #6 QueueMe → 🏆 UmbrellaDry.** C1 tied (both name a specific app
  and its useless function). C2: both are deadpan-perfect mismatches — a verdict about
  a checkout line you already left, versus a weather report about an umbrella that
  dried hours ago; UmbrellaDry's "check on it emotionally" lands the joke on a human
  truth, which the brief rewards. C3 tied (both name their user). C5: UmbrellaDry
  leaves the most vivid picture — one person, one umbrella, one pointless text,
  four to six hours too late.

## Winner

> **UmbrellaDry texts you "your umbrella is now dry" four to six hours after every rainstorm, for people who own exactly one umbrella and check on it emotionally.**

158 characters, one sentence — inside the 240-character gate with room to spare.
